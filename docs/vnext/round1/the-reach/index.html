        How to drive it

        This is a working prototype, not a picture. Everything below works.

        • Reach. Move the pointer along the thread — the marks nearest your hand lean toward it, the thread follows them, and the nearest service's rows light up in the shared facts table. Keyboard: Tab into the thread, then ↑ ↓ to walk the reach from mark to mark.
        • Carry a fact across the seam. Drag any fact onto your side, or focus it and press Enter. One carry moves it in three places at once: the thread, the shared facts row (its permissions are re-read), and the headline.
        • Play something. The Tape Deck inside Worlds plays the two new episodes. Playing one resolves a fact, which updates the queue, the ledger and the headline.
        • Esc abandons a carry in flight. Every control is a real button, and every button is at least 44 × 44 px.
