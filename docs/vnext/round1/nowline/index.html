Worlds — one instrument, swept across the morning

Worlds doesn't hand you a page of tiles. It hands you a strip chart of when it last heard from each service, and one hand you sweep across the morning. Everything left of the hand is what Worlds knew as of that moment; everything right of it simply hadn't arrived yet. Nothing is invented to look fuller.
