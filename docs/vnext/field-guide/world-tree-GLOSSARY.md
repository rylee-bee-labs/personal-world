# World Tree glossary and guardrails

The ecological vocabulary is a **human-facing overlay**. It does not replace machine semantics, authority language, repository identity, or product-owned domain language.

## Terms

| Term | Estate meaning | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **World Tree (estate)** | ecological/topological model for how the Rylee-Bee estate relates | a new orchestrator, runtime, authority source, or universal ontology |
| **Hive / bee** | Hive Works exploration and exploratory workers | permission to execute or approve consequential work |
| **Nectar** | raw findings/evidence/ideas | reviewed truth |
| **Honey** | useful synthesis from exploratory work | automatic promotion to Lore or operational authority |
| **Ant / Ant Works** | replaceable Guide staffing for tracing evidence, exploring when needed, and leaving followable evidence-backed trails | Project Home, a new authority source, or a permanent product area |
| **Field Guide** | human-readable projection of established evidence-backed trails | canonical truth, a database, or an authority layer |
| **Hill Works** | machinery for turning repeated, proven routes into maintained usable tunnels | exploration strategy, human authority, or another permanent navigation door |
| **Lab CLI** | Hill Works interface to bounded, maintained tunnels through estate machinery | Hive Works itself or a source of approval authority |
| **Worlds** | replaceable experience/theme package over the stable product skeleton | a structural role, authority source, or canonical truth for what it displays |
| **Bird** | game or expressive experience supported by the estate | infrastructure dependency |
| **Acorn** | portable seed/output that can grow elsewhere | every exported artifact |
| **Ring** | Rylee Lore as reviewed durable history | any old record or log |
| **Root** | a foundational mechanism deeper than one current application | a product that owns all foundations |
| **Vine** | the estate's integration relationship to an external/cloud service | ownership of the external provider |

A useful distinction: durable **Git history** may be treated as root-like foundation, while the estate's **GitHub connection** is a vine to an external provider. The metaphor follows the relationship, not the brand name.

## Known collision: World Tree already exists in VEFR

VEFR already uses **world tree** as part of its Norse/domain language and narrative/export model.

Keep the two meanings scoped:

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **World Tree (estate)** | this ecological map of the whole Rylee-Bee estate |
| **VEFR world tree** | VEFR's existing domain/narrative concept and export language |

The estate overlay does not rename, absorb, or reinterpret VEFR's term.

When ambiguity is possible, qualify the phrase.

## Language boundary

The estate already has stronger language rules than this metaphor, and they win.

1. **Machine/shared meaning:** Play-Nice and other owning systems retain their established semantics.
2. **Authority:** accepted estate decisions retain their exact authority boundaries.
3. **Product words:** products keep their own domain language.
4. **Product skeleton:** Home, Explore, Needs You, and Guide retain stable human jobs independent of staffing or theme.
5. **Presentation:** ecological words, workers, and theme packages may help humans orient, reason, and create art after the underlying truth is known.
6. **No reverse parsing:** do not derive machine state or authority from the ecological label.

A useful test:

> **If the metaphor disappeared tomorrow, would the architecture still be precise and operable?**

The answer must be yes.

## Adoption rule

Do not assign a new ecological role merely to complete the picture.

Adopt a term only when it predicts or clarifies useful architecture, such as:

- lifecycle
- replaceability
- authority boundary
- ownership
- information flow
- resilience
- portability
- human experience

Unclaimed ecological space is healthy. The forest does not need labels on every leaf.
