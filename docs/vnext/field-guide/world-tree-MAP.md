## 🐝 Hives: Hive Works

**Canonical role:** exploratory thinking, research, associations, critiques, findings, and candidate proposals.

Bees may wander. They can investigate, compare, fail, disagree, or return with something surprising.

- **Nectar:** raw findings, evidence, ideas, observations.
- **Honey:** synthesis that has become useful to the estate.

The bee is replaceable. Useful honey should survive the worker that gathered it.

**Core instinct:** Explore. Gather. Synthesize. Bring something useful home.

Canonical shorthand remains: **Hive thinks.**

## 🐜 Guide staff: Ant Works

**Canonical product job:** understanding and continuity.

Ant Works may staff Guide by tracing evidence, mapping relationships, carrying receipts, maintaining understandable paths, and stopping cleanly at UNKNOWN.

Individual ants are replaceable workers. Their metaphor does not grant authority.

Project Home remains separate machinery beneath consequential **Needs You** actions and remains the canonical human authority boundary.

**Core instinct:** Explore when needed. Trace. Explain. Leave a followable path.

Some ants are explorers. Their architectural value is that exploration leaves an evidence-backed trail behind rather than disappearing as one-off discovery. Established trails may be projected into the **Field Guide**.


## ✨ Experience themes: Worlds and future packages

**Worlds is an experience/theme package over the stable product skeleton, not a structural estate role.**

A theme may dress Home, Explore, Needs You, and Guide with characters, environments, metaphor, copy, motion, and other personality while preserving the same capability contracts, authority, evidence, accessibility semantics, and UNKNOWN states.

Worlds is valuable even when its only job is to make designing and inhabiting the product more energizing.

**Core instinct:** Make the stable skeleton delightful without making the costume load-bearing.


## 🐦 Birds: games and expressive experiences

Games are living, expressive things supported by the ecosystem rather than structural dependencies of it.

Examples include Cottage of the Breeze, Lanternwake, Memomancer, Burrito Journalism, and future experiences.

Birds may be colorful, loud, strange, quiet, migratory, experimental, or temporary. Their existence demonstrates that the environment supports more than infrastructure.

**Core instinct:** Delight. Express. Demonstrate life.

## 🌰 Acorns: portable seeds

An acorn is something the estate **produces**, not another mandatory repository category.

An acorn contains enough structure, knowledge, contracts, tooling, or capability to be carried elsewhere and become useful without recreating the whole Rylee-Bee estate.

Potential acorns may include:

- reusable contracts or protocols
- portable agent runtimes
- starter Worlds
- game packages
- templates
- memory primitives
- deployment patterns
- reference implementations

### Acorn test

> **Can someone carry this somewhere else and grow something from it?**

If yes, it may be an acorn.

## 🪵 Rings: Rylee Lore

Tree rings represent reviewed experience that became part of the estate's durable history.

New growth does not erase old rings. It grows around them.

The metaphor does not expand Lore's authority. Runtime logs, scratchpads, Hive state, and game records do not become Lore merely because they are old.

**Core instinct:** Remember what became part of us.

Canonical shorthand remains: **Lore remembers.**

## 🌱 Roots: durable foundations

Roots represent foundational mechanisms deeper than whichever application currently sits above them.

This is a structural role, not a product named `Roots`.

Examples may include identity, trust relationships, durable state, Git history, provenance, backups, and cooperation/contract foundations where appropriate.

A root may be replaced. The important property is that the estate's foundations outlive today's particular app or provider.

## 🌿 Vines: connected-service integrations

A **vine** is the estate's relationship to an external or cloud service.

Examples might include connections to email, calendars, cloud storage, source control, identity providers, model providers, or other outside systems.

The external provider is **not** itself part of the World Tree. The vine is the integration seam that reaches it.

A healthy vine should make its boundary visible:

- what external system it reaches
- what data or capability can cross it
- what credentials/capabilities it receives
- what owns the integration
- how it can be pruned, replaced, or disabled
- what happens when the provider is unavailable

**Core instinct:** Connect outward without confusing dependency with ownership.

## Health questions

The ecology gives the estate a second way to inspect architectural health:

- Are the bees finding useful things, or only producing noise?
- Are the ants doing bounded jobs with clear authority?
- Are Hill stores resilient without becoming shadow sources of truth?
- Is the canopy understandable and accessible?
- Are there birds: things worth experiencing, not only machinery?
- Are new rings forming from reviewed lessons?
- Are we producing viable acorns?
- Are roots independent enough to survive product/provider replacement?
- Can a vine be cut without taking the Tree's identity with it?

If one inhabitant becomes responsible for every answer, the ecosystem is probably losing useful boundaries.


## ⛏️ Hills and tunnels: Hill Works

**Canonical job:** make repeated, proven routes through estate machinery reliable and usable.

Hill Works does not decide what should be explored and does not gain human approval authority. It operationalizes paths that have earned permanence through use.

The **Lab CLI belongs to Hill Works**. It is the structured interface through which humans and bounded workers can use maintained tunnels without memorizing the machinery underneath.

Hive Works may use those tunnels during exploration. Ant Works may discover or document routes that later become tunnels. Needs You may send an approved action through one. None of those callers owns the tunnel merely because it uses it.

> **Ant Works leaves trails. Hill Works makes proven trails reliably usable.**

Not every ant trail becomes a tunnel.

**Core instinct:** Build the route once it has proved worth maintaining.
