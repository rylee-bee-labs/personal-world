## DESIGN.md

# v4182 — Evening rounds (MOCKUP)

Status: MOCKUP design. Synthetic fixture data only (`docs/vnext/fixture/fixture.json`); nothing here is a real reading and nothing is approved. All state lives in memory (no `localStorage`); a reload returns to the initial demo.

## 1. Metaphor in one line, and the main gesture.
Worlds is a house you check on at the end of the day, and each service is a lamp the house keeps lit: **Evening rounds**. The main gesture is **walk the rounds**: read one calm sentence about the house, pick the lamp that sentence points at, and see its owner's app docked beside the facts it shares. A small lamp-keeper character (inline SVG) sits in the shell and stands for the whole world's state (relaxed, attentive with head tilted, or holding a spare bulb when empty).

## 2. Integration model: how a service plugs in, where it lives in the shell, how its facts flow.
**Start state.** Two connected services: `media-shelf` (Media Shelf, `healthy`) and `front-door` (Front Door, `needs_attention`, "Needs a human"). Each is a lamp row in the Home register with its owner named on it (Media, Home). Front Door is selected on first screen.

**Connect for `notes-sync`** (Notes Sync, `unavailable`, owner Memory). `#add-service` opens the Connect destination in flow mode: five real `<button>` steps, each labelled with its step name.
1. **Discover**: Notes Sync, "owned by Memory", kind `sync`.
2. **Credential reference**: a reference name only. A pasted secret is refused with "Worlds keeps a reference, never the secret". States: untouched / dirty / submitted-pending; validated on blur.
3. **Safe test**: running, then an honest failure: reference valid, provider answered "provider timed out". Input is kept. "Check provider now" retries (backoff 2s, 4s, 8s; after three, a copyable error id). Beside it, a web fallback "Open the provider's own page" (`https://example.invalid/notes-sync`, kind `external`, placeholder, nothing is fetched). Publish stays allowed because the local facts are real.
4. **Preview and choose**: `sync.provider-reachable` (`unavailable`), `sync.local-pending` (3 notes, `live`), `sync.last-synced` (`stale`), one checkbox each, plain text on what will be shared.
5. **Publish**: pending ("Lighting the lamp…") then settled: the Notes Sync lamp appears in the register with the chosen fact ids, plus the one connected moment (§9). Mock only; no provider call.

**Fact flow.** Home, the service surface (docked fact card) and the Agent-facts view (§3) all read one in-memory store keyed by fact id.

**Live feed.** A mock clock starts at the fixture's `generatedAt` and ticks at the feed interval (default 4s; Settings offers 4s / 10s / 30s). Ages are recomputed from `checkedAt` ("checked 24s ago" becomes "28s ago"); the text is replaced, never animated. About every 25 seconds the feed moves `media-shelf` to `stale`, labelled "simulated"; the owner-app handoff "Check now" settles it. `#live-toggle` is ON by default ("Live, checking every 4s") and pauses to "Paused, ages frozen at HH:MM:SS"; the toggle is a `<button aria-pressed>`. Reduced motion changes animation only, never data.

**`#simulate-change` (a governed change, with the actor named).** Its label reads "Simulate: you lock the back door in the Home app". Worlds never locks anything and never decides; the person does it in the owning app, and the mock stands in for that. Sequence:
1. Click. The Front Door `frontdoor.back-door` row shows the handoff control "Open Home app" (`app://home`, with `https://example.invalid/home` as web form) and the line "Locking the door is your decision, made in the Home app."
2. In the same update (optimistic), the fact reads `locked` with the word **Pending** and "Reported just now by the Home app, confirming…"; Front Door becomes `healthy`; `attentionCount` goes 1 → 0; Home, the Front Door surface and the Agent-facts row all change together; the lamp-keeper relaxes.
3. After ~1.2s it settles: "Pending" is replaced by "Confirmed by the Home app" and the live region announces it. If the feed is paused, the settle still happens (it is an action, not a tick). Reset demo (Settings) restores `unlocked`.

**Pending → settled pattern (one pattern for every action).** Instant optimistic change marked by the word "Pending" plus a small outlined-dot shape, settle in ≤1.5s to "Confirmed" or "Failed, nothing changed" with recovery. Applies to: Check now, Check provider now (retry), Publish, Simulate, Disconnect (the lamp is removed at once, a status line says "Removing Front Door…" then "Front Door removed"; an "Undo" is offered for 6s only while pending, before the removal is final).

**`#disconnect`** is visible on the first screen for the selected service (Front Door by default), opens an in-page confirm dialog naming the service with buttons "Disconnect" and "Keep it", then states what is gone: "Front Door's lamp, its facts and its app frame are removed; Media Shelf and your shell are untouched." Selection moves to the next lamp in the register and focus lands on its heading.

## 3. Navigation between Worlds and a service (phone and desktop).
Four persistent destinations: **Home, Connect, Memory, Settings**. Each has a one-line contract and defaults:

- **Home**: the rounds sentence and the lamp register; selected service beside or below it (§12). Zero services: first-run (§6).
- **Connect**: two modes. *Idle* (default when opened from the nav): a "Nearby" discover list of fixture services Worlds could plug in (Notes Sync with a Connect button that starts the five-step flow, same as `#add-service`; Nightly Backup, Garden Camera, Studio Printer and Local Weather listed with their lamp shapes and "Not part of this demo's flow, shown for its state" and no Connect button). Services already connected carry "Connected". *Flow* (`#add-service`): the five steps. Zero providers: "No providers are set up yet" with the Notes Sync discover row still present, since Memory offers it locally.
- **Memory**: where **Agent facts** lives, as its own page titled "Agent facts: what Memory holds and who can see it". One table across every connected service: fact label and id, value, source, observed time and freshness word, **Agent can see**, **Human can see**, **Who can act**. It is the third view that `#simulate-change` updates. A compact version of the same rows is also the docked fact card on the service surface, so the change is visible at once without navigating. Zero services: "Nothing is shared yet. Facts appear here when a service is published." with no rows. Footer link "Open Memory app" (`app://memory`, web form `https://example.invalid/memory`).
- **Settings**: Theme (Auto follows system, default; Day; Evening); Live feed interval (4s default / 10s / 30s); Motion (Auto follows system, default; Calm forces reduced motion); Providers (list of provider-owned services and their states; zero providers reads "No providers are configured. Nothing is guessed." with Local Weather's `emptyReason` and "Open provider settings" `app://settings/providers`); Disconnect history (this session only, with mock-clock times; empty reads "Nothing disconnected in this session"; whether a real Worlds keeps history is UNKNOWN); Demo controls: **Show all fixture states** (adds Nightly Backup, Garden Camera, Studio Printer, Local Weather as lamps, each labelled "simulated"), **Start empty** (lands on the first-run Home), **Reset demo**.

A service is opened from its lamp row. Worlds keeps its chrome (title, MOCKUP label, `#live-toggle`, the rounds sentence) at every depth. Back always returns to the invoking lamp with focus restored. Layout details in §12.

## 4. The embedded-app frame (sandbox, fallback, what the shell keeps around it).
The owner's app is drawn as a framed, inset screen with a title bar naming the owner and a permanent fallback pair: the fixture link and its web form. Media Shelf: "Open Media app" (`app://media`) and `https://example.invalid/media`. Front Door: "Open Home app" (`app://home`) and `https://example.invalid/home`. Notes Sync: "Open Memory app" (`app://memory`) and `https://example.invalid/memory`. Other services use their fixture fallback label and href (`app://settings/backups`, `app://home/cameras`, `app://studio/printers`, `app://settings/providers`). `example.invalid` links are placeholders, labelled as such, and are never fetched. Intended boundary: the frame receives no Worlds credential. Whether a browser enforces that, the sandbox flags and app messaging are UNKNOWN. If the frame will not load, say so in words (what happened, why if known, what to do), keep the fact card and the owner links, and offer "Try again" with the 2s / 4s / 8s backoff. The shell, the fact card, Back and the live state keep working around the frame.

## 5. The agent/human shared-facts model (source, freshness, who sees, who acts).
The fact card beside the frame and the Agent-facts page share records with Home. Each row has source, observed time and freshness word, **Agent can see**, **Human can see**, **Who can act**. `frontdoor.back-door`: agent `read`, human `act`, reason "Locking the door changes the external system and is the person's decision." `frontdoor.guest-code-used`: agent `none` as a named, focusable, disabled control with its exact fixture reason ("Identifying a person is human-only in this fixture; the agent sees that a guest code was used, not who."); human `read`. `frontdoor.unlocked-for`: both `read`. Governed actions hand off (approvals to Project Home, others to the owning app); the mock never decides, and the actor of any change is named on the row. Notes Sync keeps provider failure and local data separate: provider `unavailable`, pending `live`, last sync `stale`. Garden Camera's `camera.human-detected-today` shows agent `none` with its fixture reason, same control.

## 6. States: empty/first run (zero services, zero providers), quiet, needs-you, stale, unknown, disconnected.
Every state is reachable in the prototype (Settings → Demo controls, plus Connect and Disconnect), not only described.
- **First run (zero services, zero providers):** reached by "Start empty" or by disconnecting every lamp. An unlit shelf, the lamp-keeper holding a spare bulb, "Nothing is plugged in yet. Connect your first service when you're ready," `#add-service`, and no invented readings. Home sentence: "The house is empty of services, so there is nothing to check." Stages the skill's reveal (§8), once.
- **Quiet:** Media Shelf, `healthy`, steady lamp. **Needs-you:** Front Door, exact state label and `attentionReason` text.
- **Stale:** shade tilted plus the word "Stale" and the age (Media Shelf by feed; Nightly Backup under Show all fixture states). **Unknown:** Garden Camera under Show all fixture states: outlined dark lamp, the word "Unknown", its `unknownReason` and `summary`; each fact reads "Unknown" with no value, never blank. **Unavailable:** lamp with a "cord out" mark and the word (Notes Sync after Connect; Studio Printer with its "Retry last job" and `lastError`). **Not configured / zero-provider:** Local Weather: unlit socket shape, "Not configured", zero facts, `emptyReason` as copy, no placeholder value, link "Open provider settings".
- **Error:** safe-test failure and frame-load failure each say what happened, why, and what to do, and keep input. Retries back off (2s, 4s, 8s); after three, a copyable error id.
- **Disconnected:** says plainly what is gone; remaining lamps stay usable. Loading uses a still skeleton with a 15s "taking longer" note. Long names and 200-character summaries wrap without breaking layout. Status is always a word plus a shape, never colour alone. Anything not in the fixture's initial two services is labelled "simulated".

## 7. Borrowed from Homarr / Dashy / HA cards / MCP ext-apps, and what is deliberately different.
Borrow Homarr's one integration feeding UI and assistant, Dashy's open-in-place, HA cards' literal state words, MCP ext-apps' contained UI under host permissions. Different: no widget grid to rearrange, no topology or routes between services, and nothing the shell can decide; the frame is always secondary to the owner label and the facts.

## 8. Your OpenDesign starting point (skill + design system) and how you used it; which guide rules you applied.
Skill `after-hours-editorial-template` is a dark, cinematic video template, so only its mechanics are used, and only where they suit: its **staged reveal** (headline, then supporting line, then detail) plays on first-run and on the one "connected" moment, never on ordinary navigation. There is no per-lamp reveal and no Home↔service chapter wipe: those would decorate the working canvas (animation-discipline). Its after-hours baseline becomes the evening theme (`prefers-color-scheme: dark` under Theme Auto), not the default. Skipped: grain, vignette, cursor light, magenta accent, 3-second pages, video framing. Its advice to avoid `localStorage` and `confirm` is followed: confirmation is an in-page dialog.

Design system `ds-lovable`: cream `#f7f4ed` page, charcoal `#1c1c1c` with opacity-derived greys, `#5f5f5d` secondary text, `#eceae4` passive and `rgba(28,28,28,0.4)` interactive borders, 12px cards, 6px buttons, pills only for toggles, weights 400 and 600 only, 8px spacing, borders instead of shadows, the dark inset-shadow primary button. Fonts are system stacks (Camera Plain is not available and no external fonts are allowed); only the one hero sentence takes `ui-serif` as a nod to the editorial skill (flagged deviation). Lovable has no saturated accent, so state is carried by words and lamp shape and there is no colour accent. Its ring-blue focus at 50% is too faint on cream; use a 2px `#1c1c1c` outline plus its soft shadow, checked at 3:1. Dark theme values are derived from the same tokens, contrast measured on implemented pairs in both themes.

**One motion scale (single contract, numbers for builder and reviewer):**
| Use | Duration |
|---|---|
| Press, toggle, hover | 100ms |
| State confirmation (pending→settled, lamp tilt to stale, row re-order, fact value change) | 150ms |
| Dialog, mobile sheet, dropdown entering | 200ms (sheet 160ms on phone) |
| Connected moment, first-run reveal (the only staged motion) | ≤450ms total, three stages of 150ms |
| Clock tick, age text | none (text replaced) |

Easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` for opacity and colour. **Reduced motion** (system, or Settings → Motion → Calm): every translate, scale and rotation is removed and replaced by a 100ms opacity crossfade or an instant swap; the connected glow becomes a still lit lamp plus the words "Connected". Data and feed keep running.

Guide rules: decision coverage before code, state-coverage's five states plus form states, anti-ai-slop (no emoji icons, no gradient hero, no invented metrics, no lorem), animation-discipline (state-change and reorientation motion only), accessibility baseline (44px targets, keyboard, live regions created before use; one polite region for feed and settle announcements).

## 9. Signature element, hero thesis, reference lock.
Signature: the lamp-keeper and the lamp rows. One SVG character (a small lamp with a face-less, shade-for-a-head silhouette) and one 24px lamp mark per service whose shape changes with state (steady, tilted shade, cord out, outlined dark, empty socket). The lamp is the mark on a row whose whole surface is the handle into that service's owner label and fact card (see §11); it is not a standalone indicator. "Connected" is the lamp being switched on: a ≤450ms warm-up at publish, which becomes a still glow under reduced motion. Hero thesis: "Every light in the house, and who looks after it." Reference lock: Apple Home's accessory-to-home status summary and Linear's calm, keyboard-first shell (mechanics only; no palettes copied).

## 10. Risks and UNKNOWNs.
Embed support and isolation, Connect API and protocol, disconnect history, and whether simulated stale events misread as real (hence the "simulated" label). Whimsy could crowd a calm app, so there is one character, one lamp set, no confetti, and motion is limited by the §8 table. Serif hero versus Lovable's humanist sans is a deliberate deviation. Contrast must be measured on built pairs in both themes. Settings persistence across reloads is not provided in the mock. Fixture data is synthetic.

## 11. How it differs from C, and what it keeps
Keeps from C: the persistent Home / Connect / Memory / Settings shell, owner-labelled places in a fixed reading rule, the owning app docked beside a shared fact card, the exact fixture facts, permission rows and handoff wording, honest separated states, and the Connect lifecycle for `notes-sync`. Differs: C's bays are a static, flat register with no motion; this concept adds a character and a lamp mark for each service, a live clock that visibly ages and stales facts, and a connected moment. C's physical metaphor (a cabinet bezel) is replaced by a domestic one (lamps and an evening check), a warm cream and evening theme replaces its white/orange skeuomorphic system, and a Home sentence ("one thing needs you") leads in place of a bare register. Connect is five steps rather than six (credential and test are kept, preview and choose merged). C's rule that nothing animates is relaxed to calm, state-driven motion on the §8 scale that reduces to still states.

**Why this is not the round-1 switchboard, signal instrument or an indicator-light wall, and not the round-2 hall of doors.** The structure carries the difference, not the lamp's skin. (a) The register is *ordered by the rounds sentence*: what needs a person first, then unavailable, stale, unknown, then quiet, then not configured, so it is read as a to-do for the evening, not scanned top to bottom as a status panel; ties keep fixture order, and it re-orders (150ms crossfade, announced) when a state changes. (b) Each row is a handle, not a readout: it names the owner, shows state word and age, and opens the owner-app-plus-fact-card pairing, which is the integration mechanic; the lamp is 24px of the row. (c) There are no jacks, ports, patch points, cross-service routing or cables, and no service-to-service relationship anywhere. (d) A service is not a door to walk through: opening one docks its app beside facts in the same screen and Worlds' chrome stays, instead of a hall of separate destinations.

## 12. Desktop and mobile layouts
One DOM and one set of checker ids; CSS grid areas and a 720px breakpoint switch layouts, so no id is duplicated.

**Desktop (1440px; tablet around 768px is a bonus).** Three columns. A left rail holds the lamp-keeper, Home / Connect / Memory / Settings, and the MOCKUP label. The centre column holds the rounds sentence and the lamp register (services in rounds order, each with state word, age and owner). The right column holds the selected service: header with `#disconnect` and the owner links, the framed app, and the docked fact card. The top strip holds `#simulate-change` and `#live-toggle`. On first screen the selected Front Door shows with `#disconnect` visible. At about 768px the rail shrinks to icon plus label and the detail column moves below the register. Opening another lamp swaps the right column in place (no page change); on Connect, Memory and Settings the right column is replaced by that destination's content within the centre and right columns.

**Mobile (390px, designed separately).** One column, no sideways scroll, 44px targets. A bottom tab bar holds Home, Connect, Memory, Settings. Reading order on first screen: MOCKUP label and `#live-toggle` header (`#add-service` in the header too), the rounds sentence, a compact selected-service card (Front Door: state word, reason, `#disconnect`, "Open Home app"), then the mock controls with `#simulate-change`, then the remaining lamps. Opening a lamp raises a full-screen sheet (200ms rise, 160ms; crossfade under reduced motion) with Back; the compact card is the sheet's header, so `#disconnect` is the same element at both depths. Sheet order: state and facts first, "what you can do" next, and the framed app last, collapsed to a single "Show the app here" button above its two fallback links, because facts matter more than a small embedded screen. `#add-service` is also a button at the end of the lamp list. Connect steps are a vertical stack with one step visible and a sticky Back/Next row above the tab bar. Memory's Agent-facts table becomes stacked cards, one per fact, with the same six fields. Settings is a single list of rows.

Checker ids: `#add-service`, `#disconnect` (visible on first screen, confirm button "Disconnect"), `#simulate-change`, `#live-toggle`, and a visible MOCKUP label.

## Critique fixes
1. **Settings and Memory had no content; agent-facts had no route.** §3 now gives every destination a contract, defaults and zero-state: Settings (theme, feed interval, motion, providers, disconnect history, demo controls), Memory (hosts the Agent-facts page), Connect (idle discover list distinct from the `#add-service` flow).
2. **No optimistic feedback; `#simulate-change` implied nobody acted.** The actor is now the person in the Home app; the handoff control is shown; the propagated change appears as "Pending, reported just now" and settles. One pending→settled pattern covers Check now, retry, publish, simulate and disconnect (with brief Undo).
3. **Unknown and zero-provider were not reachable.** Garden Camera (Unknown) and Local Weather (zero-provider, `emptyReason`) are in the register under Settings → Show all fixture states, along with Nightly Backup and Studio Printer; "Start empty" reaches first-run. All labelled simulated.
4. **Skill choreography in the working canvas; conflicting durations.** The per-lamp staged reveal and chapter wipe are removed; the reveal is limited to first-run and the connected moment. One duration table (§8) replaces the 300ms and 400ms mentions, and reduced motion is a crossfade or instant swap, not merely "off".
5. **Lamp row too close to the switchboard.** Register is ordered by the rounds sentence, each row is a handle into the owner-plus-facts pairing, and §11 argues the difference from the switchboard, status-light wall and hall of doors.
6. **Web fallback (not counted).** Each owner link now pairs the fixture `app://` href with an `https://example.invalid/...` placeholder, including a provider page in the Connect failure.
