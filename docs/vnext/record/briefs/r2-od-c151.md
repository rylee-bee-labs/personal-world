## DESIGN.md

# c151 — The Daily Edition (MOCKUP, design only) — round 2 revision

Starting point: OpenDesign skill `field-notes-editorial-template` + design system `kami`. Data: `docs/vnext/fixture/fixture.json` only (ids, names, states, facts, permissions verbatim; nothing added). New concept, not a round-1 evolution. This revision fixes the five gaps in `CRITIQUE.md`; see "Critique fixes" at the end.

## 1. Metaphor and main gesture
Worlds is a **printed daily edition** about your own services. Each connected service is a numbered **article**; the agent-facts view is the **footnotes and colophon**; a service's own app is a **plate** (a figure set into the page).
**Main gesture: turn to a section.** Choose a numbered section from the index and the page changes to that article (a page switcher: Front / 1, 2, 3… / Notes / Add). No scrolling feed.

## 2. Integration model
- A service plugs in as an **article**: headline = `name`, standfirst = `summary`, body = its facts as a short ruled table, byline = owner app and freshness (recomputed from `checkedAt`, never `ageSeconds`).
- Where it lives: the Front page lists articles by section number in connection order; a `needs_attention` service also fills the "Lead story" slot.
- **One store, one `render()`.** Services write facts, state and freshness into a single in-memory ledger (a copy of the fixture; the fixture itself is never mutated). Front page, article, plate and Notes are all renders of that ledger. Every fact has a **stable footnote number**, assigned once in fixture order and never reused or renumbered: Media Shelf notes 1–3, Front Door 4–6 (**note 4 = `frontdoor.back-door`**), Nightly Backup 7–9, Garden Camera 10–12, Studio Printer 13–15, Notes Sync 16–18. Local Weather has no facts, so no notes.
- **Start state:** Media Shelf (`media-shelf`, healthy) and Front Door (`front-door`, needs_attention) connected.
- **Discover's source.** Discover lists the fixture services not yet connected, with fixture `name` and `state` verbatim: Nightly Backup (stale), Garden Camera (unknown), Studio Printer (unavailable), Notes Sync (unavailable), Local Weather (not_configured). **The service the built prototype adds in the scripted path is Nightly Backup (`nightly-backup`)**, preselected first in the list. The other four are also connectable and each renders per §6, so every fixture state can occur.
- **Commission an article (Connect)**, one page, numbered steps: 1 Discover; 2 Credential reference (a reference name such as `ref:backup-reader`; a secret-looking value is refused with a plain note, and nothing is stored); 3 Safe test (read-only, one-line proof printed, e.g. "Read-only check: answered, 3 facts offered"); 4 Preview (draft layout of the article's facts with proposed see/act per fact); 5 Choose what to publish (tick facts; "Publish N of M"); then "Go to press". The article appears in the index and Front page. For Local Weather, step 4 prints the fixture `emptyReason` and publishes an article with no facts (§6).

## 3. Navigation
- **Phone (390px):** masthead, then an **index strip** of numbered tabs wrapping to two rows (no sideways scroll), each 44px minimum: Front, 1, 2, 3 (grows as articles are added), Notes, Add. Selecting changes page and hash (`#/n/<id>`, `#/notes`, `#/add`); browser Back works.
- **Desktop:** left contents rail (section numbers in ink-blue), one reading column (about 62ch), marginalia column at right holding the footnote refs for the cited facts.
- **Keyboard:** index is a tablist (arrows, Home/End); in an article `[` and `]` go to previous/next section (hint in the colophon). Every control has a visible focus ring (2px ink-blue outline, 2px offset).

## 4. Embedded-app frame
The service app is a **Plate**: a ruled box inside the article captioned "Plate 2. Front Door, as drawn by its owning app." A header line states the sandbox ("sandboxed; reads nothing from this page"). Below the caption: **Open in the owning app** (`https://example.invalid/home/...`; the fixture route `app://home` shown as route text), always visible, and the only thing shown if the plate is unavailable or disabled, with the reason. Worlds keeps: masthead, index, the article's headline/standfirst/status line, and the margin footnotes. The plate's UI is a mock (Front Door: a lock panel with the back-door state; Media Shelf: a library list; Nightly Backup: a run history). **The plate is a view of the ledger, not only a sender**: it redraws when a fact changes, and its own controls post events into the ledger.

## 5. Agent/human shared facts
The **Notes** page is the footnote list. Each note: number, label, value, `source` (local / external / none), "checked 28 s ago" (from `checkedAt`), and two ruled lines, **Agent: may read / may act / may not (reason)** and **Human: may read / may write / may act / may not (reason)**. `none` prints as a struck line with its fixture reason (e.g. note 6, agent: "Identifying a person is human-only…"), not removed.
**Footnote number = one focusable control, repeated in three places** (Front page, article margin, Notes row). Activating or focusing it underlines all its siblings and announces in a polite live region (`#xref-live`): "Note 4, Back door, also on the Front page, article 2 and Notes." Every Notes row also has a "Go to article 2" link, so the keyboard route Notes → article exists; the article margin ref links back to the Notes row.

## 6. States
- **First run:** Front page prints "No articles yet", a one-paragraph colophon ("Worlds works with zero services and zero providers"), one button, Commission an article. Notes: "No notes." Discover still works.
- **Quiet:** "Nothing needs you" as the lead; articles listed without a lead story.
- **Needs-you:** Lead story slot with the article, a count word in the masthead ("One needs you"), and a **Hand off** control (see §6a).
- **Stale** (Nightly Backup): tag "Stale" plus age in words; facts carry the word "stale"; table rule dashed.
- **Unknown** (Garden Camera): values print "unknown" in an em-dash cell, never 0; the fixture `unknownReason` is printed as the article's dek.
- **Unavailable** (Studio Printer, Notes Sync): tag "Unavailable"; mixed facts shown as they are (printer status unavailable, queue live); the fixture `retry` is printed as a **Hand off: Retry last job / Check provider now** control that goes to the owning app and shows `lastError` and attempts. Worlds never runs the retry.
- **Not configured** (Local Weather): an article with **no facts table**; it prints the fixture `emptyReason` and "Set up in provider settings" (hand-off to `app://settings/providers`). No placeholder value, no notes.
- **Disconnected** (`#disconnect`, on every article): the article is replaced by an **erratum**: "Correction: Nightly Backup has been disconnected. Removed: article, its notes, its stale warning. Unchanged: its data stays with Settings." Section numbers close up; note numbers are withdrawn, not reused; a Re-commission link. Worlds keeps working.

### 6a. Governed hand-off (Req 5)
Worlds never decides or acts. Hand-off destinations: approvals → **Project Home** (`https://example.invalid/project-home/approvals`; none occur in the fixture, wired for completeness); `act` facts such as `frontdoor.back-door` and any `retry` → the **owning app route** (`https://example.invalid/home/...`, route text `app://home`). The control opens that link and says "Locking the door is decided in Home." Worlds only **records the returned result** (the owning app, or its plate, posts it back).

### 6b. The one-fact demo (Req 4)
`#simulate-change` ("Simulate: Home reports the back door locked") posts, as the owning app would, the fixture `crossViewExample`: `frontdoor.back-door` → `locked`, `front-door` state → healthy, `attentionCount` → 0. The same ledger write redraws, at once: (1) Front page: the Lead story slot disappears, the masthead count word reads "Nothing needs you"; (2) the article 2 status line reads Healthy and its table row says "locked"; (3) the **plate redraws** its lock panel as locked; (4) the Notes row for note 4 reads "locked", "checked just now", with the word **"revised"**. `frontdoor.unlocked-for` keeps its last value and is marked stale ("last observed 47 minutes"), not invented. The plate's own lock button posts the same event. A "Reset demo" link restores the fixture copy.

## 7. Borrowed vs different
- Homarr: one integration serves tile and assistant. Borrow: article and note read one record. Different: no tile grid.
- Dashy: in-place app opening. Borrow: Plate in the page. Different: caption and fallback are printed, permanent.
- HA cards: subscribed state. Borrow: the ledger. Different: footnote numbers make dependencies legible.
- MCP ext-apps: sandboxed UI. Borrow: sandbox + message events. Different: the owning app keeps its decisions; Worlds prints a hand-off.

## 8. OpenDesign starting point and rules applied
- Skill `field-notes-editorial-template`: paper background (vignette dropped, kami has no gradient), serif display, page switcher, count-up on live metric numbers (off under reduced motion; never for unknown), honest `—`.
- Design system `kami`: parchment `#f5f4ed`, ivory `#faf9f5` cards, ink-blue `#1B365D` as the only chromatic colour (≤5% of area), greys `#141413 / #3d3d3a / #504e49 / #6b6a64`, Charter/Georgia weight 500, no italic, no bold, no second accent, 1px rings, solid tint `#E4ECF5`, tabular numerals. State is carried by words, rule style (solid/dashed/dotted) and position, never colour alone.
- Guide rules: single job (read the day), hero is a thesis, one signature, no AI-indigo, no hero gradient, no invented numbers, motion off by default. Ids named below.

## 9. Signature, hero thesis, reference lock
- **Signature:** living footnote numbers (§5): focus/hover/tap a number and its siblings in the other places underline together, announced politely; a changed fact's number gets the word "revised".
- **Hero thesis:** "Every claim on this page has a number, and every number tells you who said it and when."
- **Reference lock:** the Economist's Espresso-style numbered briefing and Tufte-style marginal notes (mechanics only).

## 9a. Control ids the prototype will use
`#add-service` (Commission an article: index tab "Add" and the Front-page button), `#disconnect` (on each article, opens the erratum), `#simulate-change` (the §6b demo). Also `#xref-live` (live region), `#reset-demo`.

## 10. Risks and UNKNOWNs
- UNKNOWN: whether ink-blue-only reads enough urgency; mitigated by Lead-story position and words; check in screenshots.
- Weight 500 only: emphasis by size and letter-spaced small caps.
- Charter not guaranteed offline; falls back to Georgia.
- **Phone margin (defined):** below 760px the margin column does not exist; each article ends with an inline "Notes for this article" list (same focusable number controls, same live announcements), placed directly under the table. To be checked at 390px for no sideways scroll and 44px targets.
- Real ext-app sandbox behaviour UNKNOWN; hand-off hrefs use `example.invalid`.

## Critique fixes
1. **Plug-in names no service / source (fixed):** §2 states Discover's source (the five remaining fixture services, name/state verbatim) and the added service, `nightly-backup`.
2. **One fact is architecture, not demo (fixed):** §6b makes the fixture dependency the demo; §4 makes the plate a redrawing view; the four redrawn surfaces are listed.
3. **Hand-off has no destination (fixed):** §6a names Project Home for approvals and the owning-app route for act/retry; Worlds records the result only.
4. **Missing states (fixed):** §6 adds unavailable (retry as hand-off) and not_configured (emptyReason, no table); all fixture services are connectable.
5. **Signature without keyboard path; phone margin deferred (fixed):** §5 makes the number a focusable control with a live announcement and Notes→article links; §10 defines the phone collapse. Not fixed: nothing declined.
