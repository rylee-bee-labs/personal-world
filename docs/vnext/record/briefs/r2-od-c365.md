## DESIGN.md

# c365 — The Sentence (MOCKUP, design only) — round 2 revision

Starting point: OpenDesign skill `high-end-visual-design` + design system `user:rylee-bee-studio`. Data: `docs/vnext/fixture/fixture.json` only (ids, names, states, facts, permissions verbatim; nothing added). New concept; not an evolution of a round-1 concept. This revision fixes the five gaps in `CRITIQUE.md`; see "Critique fixes" at the end.

## 1. Metaphor and main gesture
Worlds is a concierge that opens every visit with **one living sentence** about your services, then lets you pull the detail. Services are the clauses: "Back door unlocked for 47 minutes, Nightly Backup is stale, and Media Shelf is fine." Each clause is a button.
**Main gesture: pull a clause forward.** Activating a clause lifts that service's card out of a stack and opens it beside (desktop) or over (phone) the sentence. The sentence stays and rewrites itself whenever a fact changes.

## 2. Integration model
- A service is a **clause + a card**. The clause is generated from the service's `state`, `stateLabel` and `summary` and the live facts (never Worlds-side data). The card holds its facts, owner app, status line and fallback.
- Clause order: needs_attention, unavailable, stale, unknown, healthy; `not_configured` and `disabled` go into a closing clause "and N not set up". **Cap: 3 named clauses, then "and N more"** (full list is always in the stack).
- **One store, one `render()`.** Services push `state`, `freshness` and `facts` into one in-memory store (a copy of the fixture; the fixture is never mutated). Sentence, stack, dock badge, embedded frame and Facts drawer are pure renders of it.
- **Start:** Media Shelf (`media-shelf`, healthy) and Front Door (`front-door`, needs_attention) connected.
- **Discover's source:** the fixture services not yet connected, with fixture `name` and `state` verbatim: Nightly Backup (stale), Garden Camera (unknown), Studio Printer (unavailable), Notes Sync (unavailable), Local Weather (not_configured). **The scripted path adds Nightly Backup (`nightly-backup`)**, listed first. The other four are connectable and render per §6.
- **Connect** is a bottom sheet in five steps: Discover → Credential reference (a name like `ref:backup-reader`; the field refuses anything that looks like a secret value and says so; nothing stored) → Safe test (read-only probe, result in words) → Preview (facts and capabilities offered, each with proposed see/act) → Publish (checkboxes per fact; "Publish N of M"). The new clause appears in the sentence.

## 3. Navigation (Worlds <-> service)
- **Phone (390px), no horizontal scroll at any width from 320px:** sentence on top (serif), card stack below, a floating pill **dock** (not edge-to-edge) with Home, Facts, Connect. **Every dock item, clause, pill, chip and button is at least 44px** (Play-Nice target floor, aliased as `--target-min: 2.75rem`). Tapping a clause opens its card as a full-height sheet; the dock stays; "Back to the sentence" is the first control in the sheet. Browser Back closes it (`#/svc/<id>`).
- **Desktop:** editorial split: sentence left half; stack right half; an opened card widens to hold the embedded frame; dock floats at bottom centre.
- **Keyboard:** clauses are links in reading order; Enter opens; Esc closes and returns focus to the clause that opened it. The dock is a toolbar with arrow-key movement. Visible focus ring (`focus/ring`, 2px, offset 2px) everywhere.

## 4. Embedded-app frame
A nested **double-bezel** (outer tray, inner glass) holds the service's own UI (Media Shelf: a library list; Front Door: its lock panel; Nightly Backup: its run history). A slim strip above states the sandbox ("sandboxed, no access to Worlds data"). Beneath: **Open in the owning app** → `https://example.invalid/<app>/...` (fixture `app://` route shown as route text), always visible, and **shown alone with the reason** if the frame is unavailable or disabled. Worlds keeps around it: the live sentence, the dock, the back pill, and a Worlds-owned status line (state word + freshness). **The frame redraws from the store**: it is one of the surfaces that changes in §6b. Its controls post events into the store as an ext-app would.

## 5. Agent/human shared facts
The **Facts** drawer is a table of every fact: label, value, `source` (local/external/none), freshness ("checked 28 s ago", recomputed from `checkedAt`), then two separate pairs of pills: **Agent: sees / acts** and **Human: sees / acts**. A `none` permission is a disabled pill with its fixture reason (e.g. `guest-code-used`, agent: "Identifying a person is human-only…"). A just-moved row shows "changed just now". Filter chips: All / Agent can act / Human can act / Not live.

## 6. States
- **Zero services, zero providers:** "Nothing is connected yet. Worlds works without it." with one pill, Connect a service. Facts drawer: "no facts".
- **Quiet:** "Nothing needs you." plus the quiet clauses; no urgency styling.
- **Needs-you:** the clause leads; dock badge reads "1 needs you" in words; the card has a **Hand off** control (§6a).
- **Stale / Unknown:** words plus dashed vs dotted rules. Unknown facts: "unknown, never read", never 0 or blank. Garden Camera is spoken as unknown, not offline.
- **Unavailable** (Studio Printer, Notes Sync): clause "Studio Printer is unavailable, retry is offered in Studio"; card shows mixed fact states as they are; the fixture `retry` appears as **Hand off: Retry last job / Check provider now** with `lastError` and attempts. Worlds never runs it.
- **Not configured** (Local Weather): counted in "and 1 not set up"; its card has no facts table and prints the fixture `emptyReason` plus a hand-off to provider settings. No placeholder value.
- **Disconnected** (`#disconnect`, in each card/sheet): the clause is replaced for the session by a plain line: "Nightly Backup disconnected. Gone from Worlds: its 3 facts, its card, its stale warning. Still true: its data and settings stay with Settings." A Reconnect pill is offered; Worlds keeps working.

### 6a. Governed hand-off (Req 5)
Worlds never decides or acts. Approvals → **Project Home** (`https://example.invalid/project-home/approvals`; none in the fixture). `act` facts (`frontdoor.back-door`) and `retry` → the **owning app route** (`https://example.invalid/home/...`, route text `app://home`). The control says "Locking the door is decided in Home." Worlds only **records the returned result**.

### 6b. The one-fact demo (Req 4)
`#simulate-change` ("Simulate: Home reports the back door locked") posts the fixture `crossViewExample`: `frontdoor.back-door` → `locked`, `front-door` → healthy, `attentionCount` → 0. One store write redraws: (1) the **sentence** (the Front Door clause drops from the lead, "Nothing needs you" is spoken, changed words marked "updated"); (2) the **stack card** and **embedded frame** (lock panel shows locked; status line Healthy); (3) the **dock badge** (clears); (4) the **Facts drawer** row (value locked, "changed just now", "checked just now"). `frontdoor.unlocked-for` keeps its last value, marked stale ("last observed 47 minutes"). The frame's own lock button posts the same event. A "Reset demo" link restores the copy.

## 7. Borrowed vs different (including the anti-repeat list)
- Homarr: one integration, two readers. Different: no widget grid; the home is prose.
- Dashy: open apps in place. Different: Worlds stays visible around the app (sentence + dock).
- HA cards: shared subscription. Different: each card states freshness and actor permissions.
- MCP ext-apps: sandbox + events. Different: fallback link is a first-class control.
- **Versus round-1 `the-desk`** (a plain-text sheet that is always rewritten, caret-driven, with margin notes and a journal): that concept is *one sheet and nothing else*. c365 differs on the spine: (a) the sentence is a **summary header of a shell**, capped at 3 clauses, not the whole surface; (b) its clauses are **controls that open a per-service card from a stack**; (c) the detail layer is a **sandboxed embedded frame** with a printed fallback; (d) a **governed hand-off** leaves Worlds; (e) navigation is a dock and routes, not a caret. No desk text, caret, margin gutter or journal is reused. If a reviewer still reads it as a restyle, the fallback is to cut the sentence to a one-line header above the stack, which keeps the shell intact.

## 8. OpenDesign starting point and rules applied
- **Skill `high-end-visual-design`:** Editorial Split archetype, Z-axis stack (rotations removed below 768px), double-bezel, floating pill dock, eyebrow pills. Not taken: Inter-class fonts, glass-blur heroes, gradient orbs, `py-40` padding on phone. **Dropped mandatory animations** (working tool, not a landing page): "no element appears statically", staggered/entrance reveals, scroll-triggered reveals, spring cascades on the stack. Motion kept: sheet open ≤240 ms; changed-word settle ≤160 ms; both removed under `prefers-reduced-motion` (the persistent word "updated" carries the meaning).
- **Design system `user:rylee-bee-studio` — where values come from.** `design/tokens.json` is a names-only contract and is **not** a source. The pack's `ds-rylee-bee-studio.md` says `tokens.css` is canonical but it is not in the pack. So the build declares a **provisional palette**: the Mulberry & Mint dark set that this repo already ships as the estate base (`ui/src/fd/fd.css` `:root`, from design-system `tokens/mulberry-mint.tokens.json`, default since 2026-10-12), written once at the top of the file as CSS custom properties named by Rylee-Bee roles, and read everywhere by name (no other literals):

Accent roles are not used beyond the values above. **Measured contrast** (WCAG, computed): text/primary on base 13.84, text/soft on base 8.85, text/soft on raised 7.74, action/primary on base 11.31, on-primary on action 11.31, apricot on base 10.87, focus/ring on base 6.58. No px, no `clamp()`. Dark default, light and high-contrast modes switch the same roles; the light and high-contrast values are UNKNOWN until the real `tokens.css` is available, so the prototype ships dark only and says so. Display face: system serif stack (Georgia); body: system sans; no external fonts.
- **Icons — named deviation.** The system's rule is the painted B Classic sticker set beside a word; those assets are not available offline in a single self-contained file. **Deviation:** thin inline SVG glyphs, always beside a word, no emoji. Recorded here so it is a decision, not drift.
- Guide rules: single job, hero is a thesis, one signature, no invented metrics, no indigo.

## 9. Signature, hero thesis, reference lock
- **Signature:** the sentence rewrites itself; a changed word gets a ≤160 ms underline settle (none under reduced motion, where "updated" text stays) and a polite live region (`#sentence-live`) announces only the changed clause.
- **Hero thesis:** "A house is one sentence long when it is fine, and a short list when it is not."
- **Reference lock:** Linear's issue-view calm and Apple Weather's one-line summary (mechanics only).

## 9a. Control ids the prototype will use
`#add-service` (dock Connect pill), `#disconnect` (in each card/sheet), `#simulate-change` (§6b). Also `#sentence-live`, `#reset-demo`.

## 10. Risks and UNKNOWNs
- UNKNOWN: real Rylee-Bee light/high-contrast values and the display typeface (provisional palette above, dark only).
- Sentence reads awkwardly past 3 clauses; cap enforced, checked in build.
- Real sandbox behaviour (iframe `sandbox`, postMessage) UNKNOWN; embedded UIs are drawn mocks; hrefs use `example.invalid`.

2. **Hand-off destination (fixed):** §6a.
3. **Floors/motion (fixed):** §3 states 44px targets and no horizontal scroll at 390px; settle ≤160 ms, sheet ≤240 ms, both off under reduced motion; dropped skill animations listed in §8. Not fully "off by default": meaning never depends on motion.
4. **Missing states (fixed):** §6 adds unavailable and not_configured.
5. **Uniqueness vs `the-desk` (fixed):** §7 names it and lists five spine differences plus a fallback.
