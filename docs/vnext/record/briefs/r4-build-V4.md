# V4 — Today's slips (MOCKUP)

Status: MOCKUP. Synthetic fixture data only (`docs/vnext/fixture/fixture.json`); nothing here is a real reading. A visible "MOCKUP" label sits in the top bar (desktop) and the page header (mobile) on every screen, Settings and Connect included. A variation of Concept 5 (`docs/vnext/concept5/DESIGN.md`), in the house design language (`docs/vnext/house/`).

## 1. Metaphor, gesture, signature, thesis, reference lock
Worlds is a friendly service counter: today's events arrive across it as slips, and each slip plainly says which service it came from, when it was checked, what it says, and the honest state word. Main gesture: **read the day, tap a slip → its service opens (its facts and its embedded app) → open a fact to read its journey (Source → Worlds → Agent / You)**.
Signature (ONE): **the day stream** — an ordered list of event slips under a "Today" heading, newest first, each slip a service-owned observation with a real time. Hero thesis: "Today arrives one slip at a time, and every slip still says where it came from and who can act." Reference lock (mechanics, not palettes): Home Assistant's **Logbook** — a chronological stream of state changes across independent integrations, rows literal and tappable — and Slack's **day-divided channel** — the day is the unit and the newest sits at the anchor. Dot the snail stays Concept 5's small accent at the stream's "Now" edge; not a stream item, not the courier.

7. **`#live-toggle`** pauses/resumes the mock clock, the tick, re-checks and the staleness event; data and actions stay usable; `#simulate-change` still works.

## 4. What changed from Concept 5, and why
- **Home becomes the day's stream, not a standing column of service slips.** Concept 5 organised the app around a counter you select from; Rylee asked for variations built around a different overall flow, and this one moves *time → service → fact*, so the day is the front door and reads in ten seconds.
- **The fact journey moves from hero-of-home to the deepest layer** (slip → service → fact). It is kept intact, not deleted: the receipt is still the one place a fact's Source → Worlds → Agent / You is read, and it still re-settles in order on a change.
- **Selection:** Concept 5 selected a slip and then opened a fact; V4 selects the slip in hand and opens *its service* directly. The selected service is always the current slip's, so `#disconnect` is on the first screen without anything being opened.
- **Connect becomes part of the day:** the invitation is the arrival slot, and publishing lands an **arrival slip** at the top of the stream ("Notes Sync arrived at the counter", Connected stamp, Dot's parcel). Connect is still the six-step flow and still a destination.
- **Skin:** Concept 5's Wise literals (lime `#9fe870`, off-white `#f7f7f2`) are superseded by the house Mulberry & Mint tokens — dark by default, mint `#a7e6ce`, teal focus `#72b1b1`, `--space-*` / `--radius-*` / `--type-*`; Concept 5's measured Wise pairs are replaced by the house token values and their measured report.
- **Unchanged on purpose:** the counter and its slips, the fact journey, the honest rows, Dot, the six-step Connect, the four destinations, the fixture's verbatim reasons and fallbacks, the live-feed model, the MOCKUP label.
- **Not a round-2 feed shape:** this is not a newsroom wire, a daily edition, or a read-only ledger of facts. No editor composes it; it is an ordered log of *observed* facts; and every slip is a disclosure that opens a service — not a post by a person, not typographic read-out.

## 5. Integration requirements in this flow (+ the checker ids)
1. **Plug-in, live.** `#add-service` (in the arrival slot) starts Connect for `notes-sync`: Discover → **Credential reference** (a reference only; a pasted secret is refused with "Worlds stores a credential reference, never the secret"; validates on blur; pristine / dirty / submitted-pending) → **Safe test** (run / running / passed / failed) → **Preview** (`sync.provider-reachable` Unavailable, `sync.local-pending` 3 notes Live, `sync.last-synced` Stale — never merged) → **Choose** (one checkbox per fact) → **Publish**. Real `<button>`s: `#step-back`, `#step-next`, `#run-test`, `#publish`. Publish appends the arrival slip. All in memory; no provider call.
2. **Composition.** Start with exactly `media-shelf` (Healthy) and `front-door` (Needs a human); the third is `notes-sync`. Fixture ids, names, states, reasons verbatim; nothing the fixture does not contain.
3. **Embedded app frame.** Held in the service surface's App region, headed by the owner name and state word; fallback verbatim at the frame edge ("Open Media app" `app://media`, "Open Home app" `app://home`, "Open Memory app" `app://memory`); the shell's nav, Back and status keep working around it. The frame gets no Worlds credential by intent; enforcement, isolation and sandbox flags are UNKNOWN. If it fails, the rows, the receipt and the fallback stay, with the cause "This app didn't load inside Worlds."
4. **One fact, many places.** `#simulate-change` applies `lock-back-door` to `frontdoor.back-door` and updates the slip in the stream, the service surface rows, Memory and `#attention-count` at once; Front Door → Healthy, count 0.
5. **Governed handoff.** Approvals → Project Home; other actions → the owning app; the Notes Sync retry ("Check provider now", attempts 2, "provider timed out") is an owner handoff to Memory, never an approval; "Mock handoff recorded; Worlds locked nothing."
6. **Disconnect.** `#disconnect` is on the first screen for the current slip's service and opens a confirm dialog with a "Disconnect" button and Cancel. Afterwards: "{Name}'s {n} facts and its app frame are removed from Worlds; the app itself is untouched." That service's slips leave the stream, the rest of the day still reads, and with zero services only the first-run slip is left.

## 6. The live feed (the stream's own rules)
Same model as Concept 5: a mock clock anchored at fixture `generatedAt` `2026-10-13T14:24:26Z`; first paint recomputes real ages from `checkedAt` (Front Door "checked 24s ago", Media Shelf "28s ago"); each tick (5s default, Settings 10s / 30s) recomputes every age and re-renders every surface that shows one. The stream adds two rules:
- **A tick updates in place; it never appends.** A live local service's re-check (Front Door every 20s, Media Shelf every 30s) rewrites the current slip's freshness and `checkedAt`; values do not change.
- **Only a real state change lands a slip.** The once-per-session staleness event (Media Shelf misses a re-check 90s after first load) appends "Media Shelf went stale", with "Nothing has changed since {time} that Worlds knows of." and a "Check now" that restores Healthy; `#simulate-change` rewrites the Front Door slip. Every feed-driven slip carries the word "simulated". Stale, unavailable, unknown and not-configured services never re-check and never have their word overwritten.
**Pause** (`#live-toggle`): freezes the clock, ticks, re-checks and the staleness event; the stream stays readable and actions stay live; resume restarts from where it stopped. **Propagation:** one store keyed by fact id, one render pass — slip, service surface, receipt, Memory, Settings and `#attention-count` update together.

Nothing is guessed." **Long copy:** wraps, never truncates.

## 8. The house design language (tokens, type, modes, motion, warmth)
- **Structure:** every gap and pad is `--space-xs`…`--space-3xl`; slips and surfaces `--radius-lg`, the app frame `--radius-md`, buttons and state chips `--radius-full`. Column widths are product-local layout (the house says layout is product-local); no other colour, spacing or radius is invented.
- **Type:** `--type-micro` times and captions, `--type-small`/`--type-label` state words and labels, `--type-body` slip sentences, `--type-lead` "Today", `--type-h1` a screen title. The one display size (the day heading) is composed in Worlds' own layer from `--type-h1` (×1.5) — a product heading ladder on the capped six, not a seventh token. No external fonts: body `"Atkinson Hyperlegible Next", "Atkinson Hyperlegible", system-ui, sans-serif`; display `"Young Serif", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif` (as `worlds-mulberry-mint.css` sets them). All-caps labels take ≥0.06em tracking; display takes negative tracking.
- **Colour (roles, never raw literals in the product):** `--surface-base` page; `--surface-raised` slip, service surface and dialog; `--surface-sunken` the inset frame and the dialog backdrop; `--text-primary` / `--text-soft`; `--border-line` the rule between slips, `--border-line-strong` the selected slip and the app frame; `--action-primary` / `--action-on-primary` for the ONE primary action on a screen (Connect's Next/Publish; the handoff on a fact row); `--focus-ring`. Worlds **aliases** `--focus-ring` in its own layer to the teal `#72b1b1` that `worlds-mulberry-mint.css` keeps in every dark theme — a product aliases, it never edits the token. State reads `--status-*` (`--status-needs-you`/`--status-needs_attention`, `--status-done`, `--status-working`, `--status-held`, `--status-failed`, `--status-unknown`, `--status-stale`, `--status-unavailable`, `--status-not_configured`) and the only human labels are LANGUAGE.md's six — Needs you, Working, Held, Done, Failed, Unknown. - **Modes:** dark default (`data-theme="dark"`); light follows the system when unchosen; `prefers-contrast: more` and `data-theme="high-contrast"`; `data-theme="low-stimulation"` only by choice. Copy the `:root` structural block and the dark block, and the mode blocks used. The 44×44 target minimum is **not** a house token: Worlds reads it from Play-Nice and aliases it as `--target-min` (44px), as `worlds-mulberry-mint.css` already does, and says where it came from.
- **Motion:** one curve, `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)`. A new slip enters once with opacity plus a short translation (150ms desktop, 120ms mobile); a state word cross-fades and the receipt's stations tick in order, staggered 60ms; sheets and dialogs 200ms; press feedback 2px; scale on buttons only, ≤1.03, off on touch. No looping motion; nothing animates on a plain tick except the number. **Reduced motion:** translation and Dot travel are stripped, changes swap with opacity only, the stations tick at once, "Updated just now" stays 3s, data still updates.
- **Warmth, laced in (JOY-CURIOSITY-AND-CARE):** Dot at the stream's "Now" edge — asleep at first run, one nod (200ms) when a slip lands or a state word changes, carrying the parcel on connect (400ms desktop, 300ms mobile), never a stream item; the verbatim lines above are the voice; the arrival moment is staged at the end of Connect (Peak-End), not mid-flow; quiet is possible; every delightful detail can be ignored and the job still done.

## CRITIQUE.md (another engine)

# V4 critique

1. **The day stream is a weak and internally inconsistent front door.** The brief assigns “Today's slips” a timeline/feed-led flow where the day’s events stream as slips, but V4 leads with a selected-service line and `#disconnect` before the stream, and tapping a slip jumps straight to that service. A timeline full of observations is also not the promised Concept 5 counter of service slips: its signature fact journey (Source → Worlds → Agent / You) is pushed behind another navigation layer, while the embedded app is demoted again. The arrival slot is not an observation and breaks the claim that every slip is an observed fact. **Fix:** make the chronological feed unmistakably the first reading surface, make each dated event open its fact journey directly, and retain a clear service route from there. Render “Connect a service” as an action/invitation, never as an event slip; state the distinct interaction from Concept 5 in one testable sentence.

2. **Fixture truth and the house’s state language are violated.** The brief requires fixture names, states and reasons verbatim and says never to invent data. V4 labels Front Door “Needs a human” despite its `needs_attention` state and `attentionReason` (“A decision is required; the local app will not act on its own.”); House `LANGUAGE.md` maps this meaning to “Needs you.” It also turns `frontdoor.guest-code-used` into “A guest code was used · human-only,” although the fixture value is `visitor-3` and the reason says the agent sees usage but not who. “Earlier” based on an observation older than the local day and the “went stale” slip are likewise presented as fixture-backed events although neither exists in the fixture. **Fix:** use the house human label and preserve the actual attention reason; show the fixture value only to the human with the Agent `none` reason intact. Visibly distinguish simulated additions from fixture observations and define their source/time without claiming they are observed history.

3. **First run, error and everyday settings are asserted but not designed as a usable application.** The brief asks for the complete empty → connect → composed home → embedded app → change → disconnect flow and says the welcome-led variation is skippable; V4’s first-run copy appears only at zero services, with no route or way to connect both initial services and reach the composed state. “Service drops off” and Connect errors lack an explicit recovery path/owner, and Settings is only referenced as “the five sections of Concept 5 §13,” with no V4 screen order, navigation behavior, or indication that its controls share live state. **Fix:** specify the first-run path and a visible skip/return route, including how the two starting services arrive; give each error a concrete retry/edit/owner action and preserved-input behavior; spell out the V4 Settings screen and its route back to Home, retaining shared live and motion preferences.

4. **Reactive feed behavior conflicts with the brief’s honesty and live-region floor.** The brief requires reactive live feed with propagation, optimistic feedback, transitions and pause, while Concept 5’s model explicitly says routine polls and timestamp refreshes are not announced. V4 changes the current event’s meaning in place (“Updated — Back door locked”) instead of landing a distinct event, and states the UI “re-renders every age” without specifying which changes announce or how its new feed announcements are batched. It also claims the Back Door becomes Healthy and attention reaches zero, but the fixture’s `crossViewExample` only describes a mock result, not a real handoff; V4’s visible “Mock handoff recorded” disclaimer does not resolve that mismatch. **Fix:** keep observed history immutable and add a clearly marked simulated change event; define the single-store propagation to Home, service, receipt, Memory, Settings and count, instant optimistic state and settlement, and pause/resume semantics without implying a real-world action. Announce only meaningful batched changes politely; do not announce ticks or age refreshes.

5. **Warmth and house-language adoption are mostly named, not demonstrated.** The brief asks for warmth and whimsy “laced in,” using Mulberry & Mint tokens and language, while V4’s daily feed, status line, arrival slot and “counter” copy mostly read as a monitoring log. Dot is specified at the “Now” edge, but no concrete moment makes it affectionate or curious; visual hierarchy, soft contrast, token values and actual human labels are not evidenced by the design description. **Fix:** describe one restrained, ignorable Dot/copy moment tied to a real user action, and show how the feed reads as a cared-for service counter rather than a generic activity log. Name the canonical roles/values from `house/tokens.css` and `worlds-mulberry-mint.css`, use `LANGUAGE.md` mappings, and specify both 1440px and 390px hierarchy/behavior with house spacing, type and contrast roles.
